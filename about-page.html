Our Story

Founded in Pretoria over 14 years ago, Myriad Green began as a local irrigation specialist with a vision to revolutionize water management across South Africa. What started as a small team of passionate water experts has grown into a leading provider of comprehensive water-smart solutions.

Today, we serve clients across Gauteng and beyond, combining decades of experience in irrigation, plumbing, leak detection, and sustainable water management. Our commitment to innovation and sustainability has made us the trusted choice for homes, farms, and businesses seeking reliable, eco-friendly water solutions.
