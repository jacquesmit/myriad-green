# Myriad Green — Search, AI discovery, experience and conversion standard

**Owner-approved:** 26 November 2026. **Mandatory engineering baseline** for all new or materially revised public website pages, ecommerce, journal articles, MAGOS-generated content, public tools, media and integrations. Internal MAGOS systems apply relevant data quality, accessibility, security, provenance and maintainability controls; never expose private records to search or AI crawlers. This standard does not authorize live edits or override existing approval and release gates.

## Objective
Build durable, trustworthy, discoverable and useful assets that produce qualified enquiries, bookings, sales and customer value. Optimize for people first, conventional search, AI-assisted discovery and task completion. No ranking, citation, traffic or conversion guarantee. Reuse existing architecture and verified evidence before adding new systems.

## Shared vocabulary and implementation
| Discipline | Mandatory intent |
| --- | --- |
| SEO / technical SEO | Crawlability, index eligibility, unique intent/title/H1, canonical URLs, valid metadata, useful internal links, redirects, sitemap parity, rendering and performance. |
| AEO (answer engine optimization) | Direct self-contained answers, clear definitions, question-led sections, readable tables and genuine FAQs; no fabricated answers. |
| GEO (generative engine optimization), AIO (AI optimization), AISO (AI search optimization), LLMO (large language model optimization) | Consistent entities, reliable attribution, clear context, specific evidence, dated facts, accessible text and answerable passages; no invented AI ranking factors or special markup guarantees. GEO is not geographic SEO. |
| Local / geographic SEO | Accurate service coverage, locality-specific evidence, consistent business identity, genuine reviews, relevant location/service links; avoid doorway pages and false addresses. |
| Semantic / entity SEO | Coherent topics, business/service/product/location relationships, distinct intent and natural terminology; no keyword stuffing or duplicate thin pages. |
| E-E-A-T | Demonstrate real experience, expertise, appropriate authority and trust through job evidence, technical explanations, transparent authorship/source/date and honest limitations. Not a single numerical ranking factor. |
| SXO (search experience optimization), UX/UI | Fast, readable, responsive and accessible journeys, meaningful navigation, keyboard/focus support, stable layouts and progressive enhancement. |
| CRO (conversion rate optimization) | Relevant offers, clear pricing or quote basis, actual booking/WhatsApp/call/checkout paths, minimal friction and attributable conversions; no misleading scarcity or false promises. |
| Multimodal SEO | Authentic relevant media, meaningful alt text/captions, accurate filenames, optimized responsive sizes and accessible textual equivalents; do not fabricate product or job evidence. |
| Agent-friendly design | Semantic accessible controls, meaningful labels, clear states, documented public interfaces where warranted; no unrestricted access, private data exposure or unsupported crawler claims. |

These are overlapping engineering practices, not independent official ranking systems. Apply proportionately by page type and user intent; do not force FAQs, local keywords, promotional CTAs or schema onto irrelevant pages. Additional acronyms may be added only with a useful definition, evidence and concrete acceptance test.

## Mandatory design and publication checklist
1. Identify page purpose, audience, primary intent, canonical entity, location (where relevant), expected action and authoritative source. Inspect existing competing pages and preserve working URL/redirect contracts.
2. Supply original, useful information backed by genuine evidence; distinguish observation, inference, estimate and official claim. Attribute primary sources with dates/links and retain private provenance records. No hallucinated prices, reviews, technical specifications, job photos, local facts, endorsements or availability.
3. Use semantic HTML, meaningful headings, self-contained summary/answer, natural language and accessible media. Keep critical content available without unnecessary client JS. Use existing component framework, scoped responsive CSS and tested performance/accessibility patterns.
4. Verify unique titles/descriptions, canonical/index directives, HTTP status, sitemap inclusion for eligible public pages, crawlable internal links and correct published destinations. Preserve existing article slugs on same-source updates unless an approved migration/redirect is needed. Never index private dashboards, CRM, client documents, internal APIs or confidential data.
5. Use applicable truthful structured data (Organization/LocalBusiness, Service, Product/Offer, Article/BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList or other supported types); match visible content and actual business facts. FAQ markup only for genuine visible FAQs where appropriate; no rich-result guarantee. Do not add invented schema types, ratings, hidden keywords or AI-only markup. Respect Google eligibility and crawler controls; do not assume llms.txt is required or effective.
6. Check mobile/tablet/desktop, keyboard/accessibility, image sizes, layout stability, page rendering and actual CTA/booking/checkout functionality. Evaluate Core Web Vitals using field data where available, lab diagnostics otherwise; record evidence and limitations.
7. Measure actual outcomes where access permits: Search Console indexing/query/click evidence, local profile, qualified leads, bookings, completed sales and conversion attribution. AI citations/mentions only when observed with reproducible context. No invented scores, traffic or causal claims.
8. Record source revision, change scope, exact tests, live readback and outstanding exceptions. PASS only on relevant evidence; PARTIAL/FAIL/UNKNOWN/TO_CONFIRM/N/A with reason otherwise. Do not mark all existing pages compliant merely because this document exists.

## Content-specific gates
- **Water Alert Publisher:** independently verify official source identity AND actual claims, affected areas, timing and lifecycle before publication. Treat secondary reports, inaccessible/image-only sources, uncertain classifications or unverified claims as review-only until supported verification exists. State last verified time and official link, distinguish active/recovery/expired/unknown without inventing restoration estimates, separate municipal facts from Myriad Green technical advice, provide useful original guidance, avoid scaled thin copies, retain immutable source identity and stable canonical URL, verify public readback/schema/index eligibility/sitemap and exact replay. No automatic publication on unverifiable evidence. No global cache purge by default.
- **Service/location pages:** distinct search intent, actual service scope/coverage, relevant technical advice, genuine job evidence, sensible booking/contact options; avoid mass city substitution.
- **Ecommerce:** accurate manufacturer/model/images/specifications/compatibility, verified offer/price/availability policy, transparent tax/shipping/checkout and installation boundaries; product/variant canonical and schema consistency, no false inventory claims. Existing owner-approved commercial policy does not constitute evidence of physical stock.
- **Journal/technical content:** source-linked original explanations, appropriate dates, author/reviewer where verified, relevant service links and authentic media; maintain and correct outdated information.
- **MAGOS:** use structured canonical records, evidence-backed generated outputs, permission isolation, stable identifiers, validation, deduplication, audit/readback and maintainable workflows. Public outputs must pass this standard; private workflows apply relevant quality and security requirements, not public indexing. Existing business approval/payment/transaction gates remain mandatory.
