Why We Use Cookies

At Myriad Green, we use cookies to enhance your experience across our irrigation, landscaping, water filtration, and rainwater harvesting services. Cookies help us deliver fast, secure, and personalized solutions for your garden, home, or business.

We respect your privacy and comply with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You have full control over your cookie preferences.

Cookie Categories & Preferences

Essential Cookies

Always enabled - required for site functionality

Enable core site features such as navigation, booking modals, secure checkout for irrigation and landscaping products, and basic website functionality. These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly.

Performance & Analytics

Track usage patterns, service area interest, and product popularity (e.g., Google Analytics) to improve our irrigation, plumbing, and water tank offerings. This helps us understand what content is most valuable to our visitors.

Functionality

Remember your preferences, such as dark/light mode, selected service area, saved cart items, and language preferences. These enhance your user experience by personalizing the site to your preferences.

Advertising & Social Media

Help us show relevant offers for irrigation systems, water tanks, and landscaping packages on platforms like Facebook and Google. These cookies may track your browsing across different websites.

Third-Party Partners

We partner with trusted providers for analytics, payments, and social media integration. These services may set their own cookies when you interact with our booking, checkout, or contact forms:

  • Google Analytics: Website usage analytics and performance tracking
  • Stripe: Secure payment processing for online orders
  • Facebook: Social media integration and advertising
  • Google Ads: Targeted advertising for irrigation and landscaping services

POPIA & Your Privacy Rights

Myriad Green complies with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We respect your privacy and only collect data necessary to deliver our irrigation, landscaping, and water solutions.

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Access your personal information that we hold
  • Correct or update your personal information
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your personal information
  • Delete your personal information (where legally permissible)

For more details about your privacy rights, see our Privacy Policy.

Managing Your Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences using the toggles above or through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect booking, checkout, and personalized service features.

For browser-specific instructions on managing cookies:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

For help with cookie management, see our Contact Page or call us directly.
